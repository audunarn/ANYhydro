## Defect

cast the results with `float()` for complex arrays (notably
`native_potential_minus_reference_potential`). This emitted
`numpy.exceptions.ComplexWarning` (three warnings in focused-panel-tests-e and
the same three in saved-diagnostics-a), truncated imaginary content in the
RMS, and relied on lexicographic complex ordering for extrema. FD center
gradients already used real arrays and were not touched.

- No float-cast truncation, no lexicographic complex extrema, no new
